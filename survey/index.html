Pilgrimage Travel Survey

Namaskar 🙏 We are researchers at IIT Roorkee studying how pilgrims travel to the Himalayan shrines of Uttarakhand: Kedarnath, Badrinath, Gangotri, Yamunotri and Hemkund Sahib.

Why this survey?

🛣️

Find where the journey is hard, costly or slow: long waits, many vehicle changes, tough treks

🚡

Learn which new options pilgrims want, such as the hill railway, ropeways and better buses

🏛️

Help plan safer, cheaper and more comfortable yatras in future

Section C: Last-Mile Choice Experiment

Awareness of ropeway projects:Had you heard about proposed or ongoing ropeway projects before this survey?

📋 Before you begin

  • Today: from the base camp or road-head, pilgrims walk or use a pony, palki or porter (helicopter at some shrines).
  • New option: ropeways are planned at Kedarnath (Sonprayag–Kedarnath), Hemkund Sahib (Govindghat–Hemkund Sahib) and Yamunotri. None is running yet. Badrinath and Gangotri are near the road-head, so they have no cards.
  • Each card shows: one-way cost per person, one-way time, comfort (effort and shelter) and reliability (availability and wait).
  • Your task: imagine the ropeway is running on your visit. Pick the option you would actually use.

No last-mile cards are shown for selected shrines. Please continue.

📋 Before you begin: Hemkund Sahib

  • Today: from Govindghat it is a steep climb of about 21 km to Hemkund Sahib (the road now reaches Pulna). Most pilgrims take two days with a night at Ghangaria, walking or using a pony, palki or pithu.
  • New option: the Govindghat–Hemkund Sahib Ropeway (12.4 km) was approved by the Government of India in 2025 and is not yet running. It has two sections: Govindghat → Ghangaria and Ghangaria → Hemkund Sahib.
  • Each card shows: one-way cost per person, one-way travel time, comfort (effort and shelter from weather) and reliability (whether it is available when you arrive, and the wait).
  • Your task: imagine the ropeway is running on your visit. Pick the option you would actually use, given your health, group and budget.
